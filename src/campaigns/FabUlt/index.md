Across the world, ancient powers known as the Harmonics are awakening. Kingdoms tremble as forests whisper forgotten memories, deserts bloom with impossible life, and cities rise and fall upon the winds. As the enigmatic Shattered Will moves to seize these relics of a lost age, four unlikely heroes form the Astral Covenant and set out on a journey that will span continents, uncover buried truths, and challenge the very foundations of history. The fate of the world hangs in the balance, and with every Harmonic reclaimed, a greater mystery draws closer to the light.
